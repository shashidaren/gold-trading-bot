### 2a. The cap never bound — and the one TIME exit is a weekend gap

The 240-min cap cannot fire while the market is closed, and the engine only
sees candles/ticks: **#476 SELL entered Fri 2026-10-02 20:56:02, exited Sun
2026-10-04 22:00:05** — 49.1 h wall-clock, −$1.52, on the first candle back
after the weekly close (log gap 10-02 20:57 → 10-04 22:00). This is the only
TIME exit ever recorded and it is *not* evidence the cap works: it is a
position that rode an entire weekend with its stop unenforceable in a real

filled at the open, not at 4140.75). Every genuine in-session trade closed

therefore inert on this sample** (cost ≈ 0, protective value ≈ 0): it did not
cause the ~9-min SL exits and cannot be credited with any improvement. Keep it
as cheap insurance; the real gap risk is named as a new candidate in §5(b).

## CRITICAL bug found & fixed — SELL trades blinded the risk gates

`log_trade()` now writes a `Trade_Type` column (16 fields), but production
`trades.csv` still had the pre-SELL 15-field header. Appending under the old header
**silently misaligned every field of SELL rows**: for trade #41, `Exit_Reason` read
`4394.06` (the exit price), `Profit` read `SL`, `Balance_After` read `-2.32`.

Consequences while undetected (00:11–00:47 on 09-10):
- `get_daily_sl_count()` saw **0** SLs for 09-10 (should be 1) → the daily circuit
  breaker had 3 bullets left instead of 2;
- no SL cooldown after #41;
- on restart, wins/losses would undercount (40 vs 41) and balance would reload as
  $452.72 instead of $450.40;
- `status.json` `daily_losses: 0` was already wrong (visible in this drop).

Fixes (this PR):
- **`engine.migrate_trades_csv()`** — self-healing migration: rewrites trades.csv to
  the 16-field schema (backfills `Trade_Type=BUY` for pre-SELL rows), keeps a
  `.bak-pre-migration` backup, runs automatically on engine start and before every
  `log_trade()` append. Idempotent.
- **`trade_filter.load_recent_trades()`** — loud drift tripwire
  (`Entry_Time == BUY/SELL`), so the risk layer can never fail silently again.
- **Repo data migrated**: `trades.csv` is now 16-field/41 rows and fully aligned
  (verified: `python3 tools/check_data.py` → 0 fail). Raw pre-fix copy archived at
  `archive/trades.csv.bak.20260910_pre_schema_fix`.
- `tools/smoke_test.py` **Scenario F** reproduces the exact incident and asserts the
  auto-fix (drifted file → risk gates miss the SELL SL → engine restart → counted).

## Daily-loss breaker: right tool, deployed 15 hours too late

The breaker went live 18:00 on 09-09, by which time the OLD stack had already booked
**11 SLs** that day (0W/8L hour-8 pattern repeated as 0W/3L in hours 13–17). It then
correctly halted 11 further signals (18:17–23:44, all logged). Had it been live from
the start of 09-09, it would have halted at 02:26 after the 3rd SL (#29):
forgone +$11.76 of TPs (#30–32), avoided −$27.81 of SLs (#33–40) → **net +$16.05**.
Lesson recorded: deploy risk gates with the session start, not after the damage — a
mid-day deploy mostly blocks the *recovery* sells, not the losses.

## Phantom replay of blocked signals (`tools/phantom_trades.py`)

Every skip row is a full signal the risk layer refused. The tool reconstructs each
one from the price log (reconstruction validated: floor & ATR match logged values
**100%**, RSI 99.1%; reconstructed new-session signals match the funnel counters
exactly: 4 BUY + 7 SELL + #41 = `buy_all 4` / `sell_all 8`) and simulates the trade
it would have been.

| Skip kind | Blocked | Raw phantom | Sequential (dedup: in-trade + cooldown) |
|---|---|---|---|
| SL cooldown | 54 | 21W/33L, −$12.10 | 18 taken: 8W/10L, **+$5.39** |
| Daily halt | 11 | 7W/4L, +$18.53 | 7 taken: 7W/0L, **+$27.71** |
| Blackout | 7 | 3W/4L, +$0.44 | 1 taken: 0W/1L, **−$3.39** |
| **Total** | 72 | 31W/41L, +$6.87 | 26 taken: 15W/11L (58%), **+$29.70** |

Reading (uncomfortable but honest): on this sample the risk layer blocked slightly
**more profit than loss** — the halt sat out the 09-09 evening sell-off, where every
reconstructed SELL signal hit TP. The blocked signals' 58% sequential WR vs the
taken trades' 17% is a red flag about *which* signals the filters remove, not proof
they should be removed: n is tiny, it is in-sample, phantoms carry no spread/slippage
(~$0.05–0.11/trade in real fills), and the sequential view ignores cascades (a taken
phantom would have displaced later real trades). The breaker's job is capping an
11-SL day — that it did. Watch this table as n grows.

# Win-Rate Review & Gate Changes — 2026-09-09

Review of all 37 forward-test trades (2026-09-04 → 09-09), with focus on the
clean 9/8+ window (20 trades, current config end-to-end).

## Headline findings

| Metric | Full sample (37) | 9/8+ (20) |
|---|---|---|
| Win rate | 18.9% (7W/30L) | 20.0% (4W/16L) |
| Expectancy / trade | −$1.66 | −$1.76 |
| True P&L from $500 | −$61.47 (→ $438.53) | −$35.15 |
| Breakeven WR needed (RR 1.5) | ~40% | ~40% |
| 95% CI on win rate | [9.5%, 34.2%] | [8.1%, 41.6%] |

1. **The reported equity is wrong.** `Balance_After` resets to $500 at three
   points (09-04 15:30, 09-07 08:31, 09-07 13:21) because `trades.csv` went
   missing on restart and the engine silently started a fresh ledger — $24.74
   of losses vanished, and trade numbering restarted at #1 twice.
   `status.json` says $463.27; the true figure is **$438.53** (−12.3%).
2. **Entries are wrong immediately, not near-misses.** 21 of 28 losers never
   got even 10% of the way to TP before stopping out. Lowering TP or widening
   SL would not have rescued these trades — the signal fires into continued
   selling.
3. **Regime, not levels, drove results.** All 4 wins since 9/8 came from one
   4.5-hour V-bounce window (09-09 01:31–06:05). Outside it: 0W/13L, split
   between the 09-08 grind-down day (0W/8L) and 09-09 midday chop (0W/5L).
   The EMA50>EMA200 flag stayed green the whole way down on 09-08.
4. **London open is a kill zone:** 0W/8L all-time in the 08:00 UTC hour,
   0W/2L in hour 09. The old 07:55–08:15 blackout was too narrow.
5. **Data hygiene:** the skip log only starts 09-08 02:34 (filter went live
   then); the 7 trades on 09-04 08:20–09:08 had entry ATR 0.92–1.05, below
   the current MIN_ATR 1.10 (config changed after that session); the price
   log has 79 gaps >5 min but no trade was open across any gap.

## What was validated and REJECTED

An ATR-scaled "floor proximity" tightening of `tested_floor`
(low within k×ATR of the 20-bar floor) was tested and **rejected**: it
blocks every historical win (winners are bounce candles whose lows never
come near the floor). The 0.2% floor buffer is left untouched.
See `tools/validate_gates.py`.

## Changes made (this PR)

All gates replay-validated against every historical trade
(`python3 tools/validate_gates.py`):

| Gate | Rule | Effect on 37 historical trades |
|---|---|---|
| **EMA-slope** (`engine.py`) | EMA50 must be rising vs 30 candles ago | keeps 7/7 wins, blocks 7 losses |
| **Near-EMA** (`engine.py`) | entry close ≥ EMA50 − 0.3×ATR | keeps 7/7 wins, blocks 8 losses |
| **London blackout** (`trade_filter.py`) | 07:55–08:15 → 07:55–09:00 UTC | blocks 8 losses (0W/8L hour) |
| Combined | | **7W/15L kept (was 7W/30L), P&L −$52.68 → −$16.65** |

On the clean 9/8+ window: keeps all 4 wins, −$35.15 → −$21.72.

Supporting changes:
- `engine.py`: EMA50 history deque, seeded from the log's `EMA_50` column on
  restart so the slope gate works immediately (not 30 min later); new funnel
  counters `slope_confirmed` / `price_near_ema` in `status.json`; loud
  warning when `trades.csv` is missing but `status.json` shows prior closed
  trades (the 09-04/07 ledger-reset incident).
- `tools/validate_gates.py` — gate replay validator (read-only).
- `tools/smoke_test.py` — end-to-end test of the gates on synthetic data
  (trades in uptrend+dip, blocks the same dip in a decline, restart seeding).
- Full backup of pre-change code + data: `archive/backup-20260909_pre_gate_changes/`
  and git tag `backup-pre-gate-changes-2026-09-09`.

## Honest caveats

- **These gates are damage control, not a proven edge.** Even after them the
  replayed win rate is 32% vs the ~40% breakeven. The point is to stop the
  bleeding from counter-trend dips while the forward test collects cleaner data.
- n=37 (n=20 clean) is a tiny sample and all validation is in-sample; treat
  the gate parameters as hypotheses, not truths.
- CSV schemas (`trades.csv`, `forward_test_log.csv`, `skipped_trades.csv`)
  are unchanged, so existing files remain compatible.
